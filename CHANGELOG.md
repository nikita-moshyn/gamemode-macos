# Changelog

Changes in published releases, ordered by publication date. Downloads are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/releases). The early beta numbers use a separate sequence from stable releases.

## [0.0.1-beta.3](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/releases/tag/v0.0.1-beta.3) - 12 October 2026

- Reworked the menu bar controls and settings window.
- Added the Input Guard master switch, with protection off by default on fresh installs.
- Strengthened interrupted-session recovery with durable snapshots and explicit recovery controls.
- Expanded display management to cover compatible external displays and per-display selection.
- Improved mouse preset restoration and handling of saved preferences.

[Source changes](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/compare/v0.0.1-beta.2...v0.0.1-beta.3).

## [0.1.1](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/releases/tag/0.1.1) - 12 April 2026

- Released the refresh rate, power, and logging changes from `0.0.1-beta.2` on the stable channel.
- Uses the same source revision as `0.0.1-beta.2`.

## [0.0.1-beta.2](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/releases/tag/v0.0.1-beta.2) - 12 April 2026

- Added ProMotion refresh rate controls with per-app activation.
- Added automatic Low Power Mode changes with a battery threshold and restoration after gaming.
- Added structured logging with a configurable minimum log level.
- Fixed a startup deadlock.

[Source changes](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/compare/v0.0.1-beta.1...v0.0.1-beta.2).

## [0.0.1-beta.1](https://github.com/nikita-moshyn/gamemode-macos/releases/tag/v0.0.1-beta.1) - 22 March 2026

- First published beta, with automatic activation for monitored apps.
- Included configurable function keys, system shortcuts, gestures, mouse presets, and global hotkeys.
- Included settings restoration, interrupted-session recovery, and Sparkle updates.
- Updated the app icon.
